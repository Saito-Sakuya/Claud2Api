# Third-party licenses

[中文](THIRD_PARTY_ZH.md) · English · [Documentation index](DOCS.md)

Claud2Api source is distributed under the root MIT license. Dependencies retain
their respective licenses. The locked dependency inventory, source revisions,
authors, available license texts and checksums are in
`LICENSES/dependencies/index.json` and the accompanying directories. Packages
declaring an SPDX license without bundling its text are listed explicitly.

The distribution includes native BoringSSL and SQLite components through its
dependencies. Consult their bundled notices before redistributing binaries.
Original dependency notices and the machine-readable inventory remain unchanged;
the bilingual guide does not replace them.
